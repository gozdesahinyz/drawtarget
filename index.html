The problem

Most drawdown calculators ask the wrong question first.

The usual way
"What withdrawal rate do you want — 3%? 4%?"

A question most people approaching retirement can't actually answer, because it isn't how they think about their own money.

DrawTarget's way
"How much income do you need this year?"

A question anyone can answer immediately. We work backwards from there — the withdrawal, the tax, the tax-free lump sum, and how long the pot lasts follow automatically.
