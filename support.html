DrawTarget is an independent app and is not connected to Pension Wise, MoneyHelper, HMRC, the DWP or the UK government.

Common questions

Is the target income before or after tax? After tax - the amount you want to take home each year. DrawTarget works out the taxable withdrawal and tax behind it.

Which tax rules are used? 2026/27 rates for England, Wales and Northern Ireland. Scottish income tax rates are not supported yet.

My State Pension is different from the full rate. Enter your own figure in Settings; check your forecast on GOV.UK.

How do I restore DrawTarget Pro on a new device? Settings → Purchases → Restore Purchase, signed in with the same Apple ID you bought it with.

How do I get a refund? Purchases are handled by Apple. Request a refund at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Does the app collect my data? No. Everything is calculated on your device. See the Privacy Policy & Terms.
