Privacy Policy

DrawTarget does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data. Everything you type into the app — your income target, pot values, State Pension override — stays on your device only, inside the app's own storage. Nothing is sent to us, to Apple's analytics, or to any third party. The app has no servers of its own. The only network connection it makes is through Apple's own App Store system, to show the price of DrawTarget Pro and to process a purchase or restore if you choose to; that is handled by Apple under Apple's privacy policy, and none of your calculation inputs are part of it.

Specifically:

  • No account or sign-in. The app has no login, so we never see a name, email address, or identity.
  • No analytics or crash reporting SDKs. We don't know how many people use the app, what they enter, or when it crashes, beyond whatever aggregated, anonymous data Apple itself provides to all developers through App Store Connect.
  • No advertising. There are no ads and no ad-tracking identifiers.
  • A few small settings are saved on your device using standard iOS storage (UserDefaults): a custom State Pension amount, if you choose to enter one; whether you pay income tax in Scotland or elsewhere in the UK; a record that you have read the “Before you start” screen; and, to decide when to show Apple's standard “rate this app” prompt, how many calculations you have run, the date you first used the app and the app version you were last asked on. None of this leaves your device, and all of it is deleted if you delete the app. The income and pot values you type into a calculation are not saved, unless you choose to save a scenario (a DrawTarget Pro feature).
  • Saved scenarios, if you choose to save any, are kept in a file inside the app on your device only. iOS encrypts the file while your device is locked; it is never sent anywhere and is deleted if you delete the app or swipe to delete the scenario.
  • PDF exports you choose to share are generated entirely on your device and only go wherever you personally choose to send them (e.g. Mail, Files, AirDrop) via Apple's standard share sheet.

Because nothing is collected, the usual data-subject rights under UK GDPR (access, correction, deletion, portability) don't have anything to act on — there is no record of you anywhere for us to show, correct, or delete. If you have any question about this, contact us using the details below.

If a future version of this app ever changed this — for example, to add optional cloud backup — this policy would be updated first, with a clear description of exactly what changed and why, and the change would need your explicit action to opt in.

A calculator only - what it leaves out. Results are estimates that depend entirely on what you enter and on simplifying assumptions, which are listed in full inside the app (Settings → Assumptions & limitations) and printed on every PDF. In particular, the app assumes your only income is the State Pension and your pension withdrawals; it covers defined contribution pensions only; it assumes no charges, and no investment growth or inflation unless you choose one of the illustration growth rates; and it does not consider inheritance tax, National Insurance or means-tested benefits. Choose Scotland in Settings if you pay Scottish income tax. You are responsible for checking whether these assumptions fit your circumstances before relying on any figure, and for deciding whether to take guidance or advice.

Tax year and figures. Calculations use 2026/27 UK tax year figures, with England, Wales and Northern Ireland income tax bands or, if you choose Scotland in Settings, Scottish bands. Figures such as the Personal Allowance, tax thresholds, the Lump Sum Allowance, and the full State Pension amount are set by government policy and change - most reliably every April, sometimes mid-year. We aim to keep these current but do not guarantee they reflect the very latest announcement at every moment; always cross-check anything significant against gov.uk or an adviser before acting on it.
